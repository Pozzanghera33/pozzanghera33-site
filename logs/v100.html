🌊 Welcome to the Daily Chaos

DEAD BEFORE WORK: THE COMMUTE is a dark comedy ragdoll-survival game where everything that can go wrong... absolutely will.

Meet Archibald "Archie" L. Dice, a man whose luck is so catastrophically bad that even his morning commute becomes a battlefield of malfunctioning machines and chain-reaction disasters.
