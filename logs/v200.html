🎉 MAJOR UPDATE v2.0.0 IS NOW LIVE!

Introducing "WORDJONG" Mode & Unrestricted Sandbox Mode!

Version 2.0.0 is our biggest content update yet! We’ve added entirely new ways to play, experiment, and suffer hilariously during Archie’s endless morning commute.

  • 🔤 WORDJONG (ワージャン) Mode: A unique word-puzzle game mode combining English vocabulary assembly with mahjong-style tile mechanics! Spell words, hit massive chain combos, and earn bonus GAG POINTS!
  • 🏗️ Sandbox Mode: Build without boundaries! Unlimited trap budget, free object scaling, and zero constraints to design the ultimate obstacle course.
  • 🌐 Expanded Stage Sharing & Online Rankings: Seamlessly exchange custom stages across platforms via Stage Codes or Steam Workshop, and climb global leaderboards!
  • 👗 New Outfits & Absurd Traps: Unlock fresh corporate attire and even more unreasonable workplace hazards using earned GAG POINTS.

🌊 Welcome to the Daily Chaos

DEAD BEFORE WORK: THE COMMUTE is a dark comedy ragdoll-survival game where everything that can go wrong... absolutely will.

Meet Archibald "Archie" L. Dice, a man whose luck is so catastrophically bad that even his morning commute becomes a battlefield of malfunctioning machines, rogue vehicles, and chain-reaction disasters.
