NEW SYSTEM LIVE

📬 Connection & Rewards: The In-Game Message Box!

To deepen our connection with all of you who play and support our game, we have officially introduced the In-Game Message Box System!

(Think of it like the Inbox feature in Dead by Daylight, which is currently celebrating its fantastic 10th anniversary!)

Key Features & Perks:

  • Patch Notes & News: Easily check recent updates and mechanical changes directly in-game.
  • Massive Wardrobe Expansion: We have added **9 brand-new outfits** to the game (including exclusive Career Board rewards)!
  • Early Access Supporter Gift: As a huge thank you to our incredible early supporters, we are gifting you **2 exclusive outfits**! You can redeem them instantly by hitting the "CLAIM" button inside this new Message Box. Don't miss out!

📈 The "Career Board" is Here: Earn EXP & Climb the Corporate Ladder!

Our next massive systemic expansion introduces a proper EXP Progression System! Every single time Archie (you) tackles the brutal morning rush hour, you will accumulate valuable experience points.

With the newly established "Career Board" (styled beautifully like a traditional board game or a stamp rally), you get a reward for **every single level-up**! As you climb your way up the corporate ladder, your official **job title/corporate rank will dynamically change**.

🎁 Exclusive Corporate Milestones!

Reaching specific major milestones on the board will unlock highly valuable, exclusive Outfits and unique Traps that *cannot* be unlocked anywhere else in the game! Furthermore, your current **Creator Name** can now be officially checked and verified right here on the board interface.

The Pro-Tip for Maximum EXP: The secret to multiplying your experience gains is triggering massive, high-scoring Trap Combos!
(Example Combo String: Taxi Crash ➔ Manhole ➔ Steam Vent ➔ Manhole Again ➔ AC Outdoor Unit)

Conquer the Career Board, secure your corporate status, and claim all the limited-edition gear!

LEVEL EDITOR EXPANSION

🛠️ Level Editor: Dynamic Memory Allocation & "NewYork ALLEY02"

Architects, rejoice! We are officially adding a brand-new foundation map to the Level Editor: "NewYork ALLEY02"!

This specific environment focuses heavily on tight, narrow corridors and claustrophobic sprints. Because spacing is limited, players will need a much more sophisticated, highly calculated trap configuration to get through.

⚙️ Dynamic Memory Scaling Upgrades: To heavily optimize our foundation layouts, the **default initial memory limit is now dynamic and scales automatically based on the size of each stage**! (This design ensures larger maps naturally receive a higher baseline memory limit to give you extra breathing room).

Rest assured, **any extra memory slots you purchased and unlocked in the store will be added directly on top of this dynamic base**, meaning your shop progress remains completely untouched and exactly the same. I completely believe your hands will birth even more beautifully chaotic masterpieces!
