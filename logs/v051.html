UPCOMING SYSTEM PREVIEW

📬 Connection & Rewards: The In-Game Message Box!

To deepen our connection with all of you who play and support our game, we are officially introducing the In-Game Message Box System!

(To give you a quick idea, think of it like the Inbox feature in Dead by Daylight, which is currently celebrating its fantastic 10th anniversary!)

Key Functions Include:

  • Patch Notes & News: Easily check recent updates and mechanical changes directly in-game.
  • Seasonal Drops: Claim limited-time seasonal traps and custom outfits!
  • Early Access Perks: Exclusive trap unlocks and special outfits dedicated entirely to our early supporters. Isn't that wonderful?

📈 The Promotion Track: Earn EXP & Climb the Career Ladder!

Our next massive systemic expansion introduces a proper EXP Progression System! Every single time Archie (you) tackles the brutal morning rush hour, you will accumulate valuable experience points.

As your EXP grows, Archie will climb the corporate "Career Ladder" (Promotion Track), styled beautifully like a traditional board game or a stamp rally. Moving across the spaces on the board will trigger major corporate promotions!

🎁 Reach Milestones to Unlock Corporate Loot!

Specific spaces on the track hold highly valuable rewards, allowing you to intercept exclusive traps and unique character outfits.

The Pro-Tip for Maximum EXP: The secret to multiplying your experience gains is triggering massive, high-scoring Trap Combos!
(Example Combo String: Taxi Crash ➔ Manhole ➔ Steam Vent ➔ Manhole Again ➔ AC Outdoor Unit)

Conquer the Promotion Track, secure your corporate status, and claim all the limited-edition gear!

LEVEL EDITOR EXPANSION

🛠️ Level Editor: New Sandbox Stage "NewYork ALLEY02"

Architects, rejoice! We are officially adding a brand-new foundation map to the Level Editor: "NewYork ALLEY02"!

This specific environment focuses heavily on tight, narrow corridors and claustrophobic sprints. Because spacing is limited, players will need a much more sophisticated, highly calculated trap configuration to get through.

Due to the vast structural length of the map, the initial available default memory limit has been raised to give you extra breathing room. Combined with the memory unlock slots available in our recently deployed trap lanes, I completely believe your hands will birth even more beautifully chaotic masterpieces!
