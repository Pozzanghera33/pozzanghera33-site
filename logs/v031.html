Hi everyone! MASTASHIO here from POZZANGHERA 33.

It’s been another intense week of diving deep into the development "puddle." I’ve been working on some major gameplay shifts that will change how you experience Archie's daily disasters. Today, I’m excited to unveil the details for the upcoming Ver 0.3.1 update!

EVOLUTION v0.3.1

💥 The Art of the Blast: Quadruple BOOM!

Until now, Archie was limited to only 2 "BOOM" (blasts) once he entered Ragdoll Mode. That changes now. In this update, by successfully chaining trap combos, you can now trigger up to 4 consecutive blasts!

This isn't just about high scores—it’s about artistic chaos. With these extra boosts, your skill determines how gracefully (and hilariously) Archie sails through the air. Chain different traps together to unlock these extra blasts and watch your score—and Archie—skyrocket.

NEW HAZARD

🏙️ NY Style Steamed Trap

Straight from the streets of New York! We’ve added rhythmic steam vents to the environment. These vents release high-pressure steam at set intervals—time it wrong, and Archie gets a one-way ticket to the stratosphere. It's the perfect tool for starting a massive aerial combo.

✨ UI & Visual Effects

We’ve added subtle details to make the world feel more alive and the mechanics more intuitive:

  • GO Icon System: A new visual cue that shows when your BOOM is ready. The icon flashes when your window to trigger a follow-up blast is closing!
  • Heat Distortion: Added shimmering exhaust effects to vehicle tailpipes for that extra touch of urban realism.

🛠️ Full Patch Notes: Ver 0.3.1 (Coming Soon)

  • Max BOOM Increased: Blast count limit raised from 2 to 4 (requires combos).
  • Combo Scoring: Triggering different traps in one flight now grants bonus points.
  • New UI: Added "GO" icon with flashing timer logic for secondary blasts.
  • New Trap: NY Style Steamed Trap (Interval-based steam hazard).
  • VFX Update: Added shimmering exhaust heat effects to cars.
