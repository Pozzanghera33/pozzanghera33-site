Weekly Progress Report

Hi everyone! I’m MASTASHIO, the creator behind POZZANGHERA 33.

Following our v0.2.0 update where we introduced two new stages, I’m currently working hard on v0.2.1, which will add yet another challenging level to your commute!

🚛 The Inspiration: Cinematic Chases

The upcoming stage features a massive trailer truck chasing you, utilizing full physics-based chaos. I’ve always been a huge fan of the relentless tension in Steven Spielberg’s "Duel" (1971) and the iconic car chase from the anime "Lupin III: The Mystery of Mamo".

While this isn't a direct homage, I felt that being chased by a giant truck is an essential "rite of passage" for any instant-death runner!

✨ Comical but Tense

As always, this game is designed to be enjoyed by all ages, so there is no gore involved. However, I want to balance the comical gags with moments of genuine, heart-pounding tension. This truck stage is the perfect example of that "funny but terrifying" vibe.

Stay tuned—you'll be able to play it very soon!
