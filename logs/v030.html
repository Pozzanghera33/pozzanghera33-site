CORE UPDATE v0.3.0

👗 The Character Fitting Room (Experimental)

The wait is over—Archie can finally dress for success (or inevitable disaster)! We are introducing the **Character Fitting Room**, allowing you to customize Archibald "Archie" L. Dice’s appearance.

"The fitting room is still under construction, but I wanted you to try it first!"

  • Experimental Access: This is an early alpha version of the customization system where categories and UI layouts are not yet final.
  • Express Your Style: Customizing Archie makes his high-speed "fly-offs" even more spectacular and original.
  • Shop & Equip: Use your hard-earned in-game money to purchase and wear new items immediately.

*We wanted you to experience this Experimental feature as early as possible, even while the final display format is still evolving!
